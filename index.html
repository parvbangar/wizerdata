Generic global news APIs

A firehose of articles

  • Indian coverage arrives in batches, often hours after publication
  • One article per row: reprints of the same wire story look like separate news
  • Hindi is an afterthought, scored by English-first models
  • Companies tagged by name, or by a US ticker for an Indian stock
  • Accuracy claims without a test set behind them
WIZER Data

Stories, ready to use

  • Publisher sitemaps read every 2 minutes. Median 39 minutes ahead of GDELT
  • Every article belongs to a story, with volume, velocity and the top 5 links
  • English and Hindi in one embedding space, so both join the same story
  • Companies resolved to NSE symbol, BSE code and ISIN
  • Every accuracy number published with its gold set and date
